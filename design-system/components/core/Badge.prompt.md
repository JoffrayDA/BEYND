Small status labels + the discreet gamification unit (level + XP).

```jsx
<Badge tone="primary">En cours</Badge>
<XPPill xp={240} level={3} />
```

Per brief: "éléments gaming (XP light + badges) présents mais discrets" — XPPill is intentionally small, low-contrast neutral background with just a level-number accent dot. Never a full progress-bar/quest-log treatment.
